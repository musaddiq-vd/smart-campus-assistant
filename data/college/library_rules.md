# Central Library Rules

Rules of the Central Library of XYZ College of Engineering (fictional). The Librarian is Mrs. Shalini Kher (fictional). Email: library@xyz.edu.example.

## About the library

The Central Library has about 62,000 books, 180 print journals and access to 4,500 e-journals and e-books through the (fictional) Digital Engineering Library consortium. It has a reading hall with 400 seats, 40 computers for e-resources, and a separate quiet zone. Every book has a book ID such as LIB-CS-0421, where the middle part is the subject (CS, EC, ME, CE, MA, GN).

## Library timings

| Day | Timing |
|---|---|
| Monday to Saturday | 08:00 to 22:00 |
| Sunday | 09:00 to 17:00 |
| During end-semester exams (2026-12-01 to 2026-12-10) | 08:00 to 24:00 every day |

The library is closed on national holidays.

## Membership

Every student is a library member from the day of admission. The student ID card is the library card. Members log in to the library portal with their student ID to see the books issued in their name, due dates and fines.

## Borrowing limits

| Member type | Maximum books | Loan period |
|---|---|---|
| B.Tech student | 4 books | 14 days |
| Final-year B.Tech student during the project semester | 4 books plus 2 project books | 14 days |
| Faculty | 10 books | 60 days |

- Reference books, journals and the latest edition of textbooks marked "Reference" are not issued; they can be read in the library.
- A book can be renewed once for another 14 days if no one has reserved it. Renewal is done online or at the counter before the due date.

## Overdue fine

- A fine of Rs 5 per day per book is charged after the due date.
- The fine for one book is capped at Rs 500.
- While a student has an overdue book, no new book is issued.
- Fines can be paid online on the student portal (UPI or Net Banking) or at the library counter.
- A student with a library fine above Rs 200 or any overdue book will not get the end-semester hall ticket until the fine is paid and the books are returned.

## Lost or damaged books

- Report a lost book to the library counter as soon as possible. The overdue fine stops on the day the loss is reported.
- The student can replace the lost book with a new copy of the same or a later edition, plus a processing charge of Rs 100.
- If the book cannot be replaced, the student pays twice the current price of the book plus Rs 100 processing charge.
- A damaged book (torn pages, water damage) is treated like a lost book.

## Reservation

A book that is issued to someone else can be reserved online. The reserving student is informed by email to xyz.edu.example and has 2 days to collect it.

## No-dues certificate

Final-year students must return all books and pay all fines to get the library no-dues certificate, which is needed for the degree certificate and the caution deposit refund.

## Code of conduct

- Silence in the reading hall. Mobile phones on silent mode.
- No food or drinks inside, except water bottles.
- Bags are kept at the property counter.
- Misuse of e-resources (mass downloading) leads to suspension of library membership for one semester.
