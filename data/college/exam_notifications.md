# Exam Notifications Odd Semester 2026-27

Official examination notices of XYZ College of Engineering (fictional) for the Odd semester 2026-27 (Semesters 1, 3, 5 and 7). Issued by the Controller of Examinations, Dr. Nikhil Bhatia (fictional). Email: exams@xyz.edu.example.

## Notice EX-2026-14 (dated 2026-08-25): Mid-semester examinations

- Mid-semester examinations for all branches and semesters were held from 2026-09-14 to 2026-09-19.
- Each mid-semester paper is 90 minutes long and carries 30 marks (30% of the course total).
- Mid-semester marks were published on the student portal on 2026-10-05.
- There is no re-examination for a missed mid-semester exam, except on medical grounds approved by the Dean Academics within 7 days.

## Notice EX-2026-21 (dated 2026-10-26): End-semester examination schedule

- End-semester examinations for the Odd semester 2026-27 will be held from 2026-12-01 to 2026-12-10 for all branches (CSE, ECE, ME, CE).
- Every branch starts its end-semester exams on Tuesday 2026-12-01. The last paper is on Thursday 2026-12-10.
- Morning session: 10:00-13:00 for Semester 1 and Semester 5.
- Afternoon session: 14:00-17:00 for Semester 3 and Semester 7.
- Each end-semester paper is 3 hours long and carries 50 marks (50% of the course total). Internal assessment carries the remaining 20%.
- Students must reach the exam hall 20 minutes before the start. Entry is not allowed more than 30 minutes after the start.
- Mobile phones, smart watches and programmable calculators are not allowed in the exam hall.

## Notice EX-2026-24 (dated 2026-11-02): Hall tickets

- End-semester hall tickets will be released on the student portal on 2026-11-25.
- A hall ticket is issued only if the student has (a) no unpaid fee dues or late fee, (b) no library fine above Rs 200 and no overdue books, and (c) at least 75% attendance in the course, or an approved condonation (65% to 75%).
- Students with attendance below 65% in a course are debarred from the end-semester exam of that course and get the grade "X" (must re-register).
- The attendance shortage list will be published on 2026-11-23. Condonation applications close on 2026-11-24.
- Carry the printed hall ticket and the college ID card to every exam.

## Notice EX-2026-25 (dated 2026-11-02): Results and revaluation

- End-semester results will be declared on or before 2026-12-31 on the student portal.
- Students can see their answer scripts on 2027-01-04 and 2027-01-05.
- Revaluation: apply online within 7 days of the result declaration. Fee: Rs 750 per course. Revised results are published within 15 days.
- If revaluation changes the marks by 10% or more of the maximum marks, the revaluation fee is refunded.

## Notice EX-2026-26 (dated 2026-11-02): Supplementary examinations

- Supplementary examinations for students with F grades (backlogs) or absent grades will be held from 2027-01-18 to 2027-01-23.
- Registration on the portal: 2027-01-04 to 2027-01-10. Fee: Rs 500 per course.
- The maximum grade in a supplementary exam is "A" (8 points). Internal assessment marks are carried forward.
- Students debarred for low attendance (grade "X") cannot write the supplementary exam; they must re-register for the course.

## Examination rules summary

- Malpractice (copying, using a phone, impersonation) leads to cancellation of all papers of that semester.
- A student absent from an end-semester exam without approved medical leave gets grade "Ab", which counts as a backlog.
