# Academic Rules for B.Tech Students

These are the academic rules of XYZ College of Engineering (fictional), approved by the (fictional) Academic Council for students admitted in 2022 and later. The Dean Academics is Dr. Pradeep Saini (fictional), email dean.academics@xyz.edu.example.

## Credits and duration

- The B.Tech is an 8-semester programme. A student must earn 160 credits to graduate.
- The maximum duration is 12 semesters (6 years) from admission, or 10 semesters for lateral entry students.
- A normal semester load is 18 to 22 credits. A student with backlogs may register for up to 26 credits including backlog courses.

## Attendance

- Minimum attendance is 75% in every course (theory and lab counted separately).
- Attendance is calculated from the first day of classes (2026-07-20 for the Odd semester 2026-27) up to the last instruction day (2026-11-20).
- Condonation: a student with attendance from 65% up to 75% in a course may apply for condonation with a valid medical certificate or an approved reason (sports, cultural or college duty). The condonation fee is Rs 500 per course. The Dean Academics decides each case.
- A student with attendance below 65% in a course is debarred from the end-semester exam of that course, gets the grade "X", and must re-register for the course in a later semester.
- Attendance on the student portal is updated every Monday. Students below 75% receive an email warning on 2026-10-15 and 2026-11-15.

## Assessment

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Internal assessment (quizzes, assignments, lab work) | 20% |
| Mid-semester examination | 30% |
| End-semester examination | 50% |

To pass a course a student needs at least 40% of the total marks and at least 35% in the end-semester exam.

## Grading scale

| Marks (%) | Grade | Grade points |
|---|---|---|
| 90-100 | O (Outstanding) | 10 |
| 80-89 | A+ (Excellent) | 9 |
| 70-79 | A (Very good) | 8 |
| 60-69 | B+ (Good) | 7 |
| 50-59 | B (Above average) | 6 |
| 45-49 | C (Average) | 5 |
| 40-44 | P (Pass) | 4 |
| Below 40 | F (Fail) | 0 |
| Absent | Ab | 0 |
| Debarred for attendance | X | 0 |

## SGPA and CGPA

- SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) = sum of (course credits x grade points) / total credits registered in the semester.
- CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) = the same weighted average over all semesters completed so far.
- Conversion to percentage: Percentage = (CGPA - 0.5) x 10. For example, a CGPA of 8.12 is 76.2%.
- Classification at graduation: First Class with Distinction for CGPA 8.0 or more with no backlog ever; First Class for CGPA 6.5 or more; Second Class for CGPA 5.5 to 6.49; Pass Class below 5.5.

## Backlogs

- A course with grade F, Ab or X is a backlog until it is cleared.
- F and Ab grades can be cleared in the supplementary examination (held in January and June) or by re-registering in a later semester. X grades can only be cleared by re-registering.
- A student with more than 6 active backlogs at the end of Semester 6 cannot register for Semester 7 (year back).
- Students with active backlogs are not eligible for campus placements until the backlogs are cleared (see the Placements 2026 document) and cannot get the Distinction class.

## Promotion and year back

- A student is promoted to the next year if they earned at least 50% of the credits offered in the current year.
- A student who is not promoted repeats the year (year back). The student ID does not change. For example, a student admitted in 2022 who had a year back is now in the fourth year (Semester 7) in 2026-27.

## Branch change

- Allowed only once, at the end of the first year (after Semester 2).
- Eligibility: CGPA of 8.5 or more at the end of Semester 2, no backlogs, and no disciplinary action.
- A branch change is possible only if the target branch has a vacancy, and the branch may not go above its approved intake. At most 10% of the intake of any branch can leave it through branch change.
- Applications open on 2027-06-10 and results are announced on 2027-07-05. Seats are allotted in order of CGPA.

## Academic integrity

Plagiarism in assignments or projects leads to zero marks for that component on the first offence and a grade F in the course on the second offence. Exam malpractice rules are in the Exam Notifications document.

## Leave

Medical leave of more than 3 days must be reported to the class mentor within 7 days with a medical certificate. Medical leave does not count as attendance but is considered for condonation.
