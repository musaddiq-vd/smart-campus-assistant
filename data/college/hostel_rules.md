# Hostel Rules and Facilities

Hostel rules of XYZ College of Engineering (fictional) for 2026-27. The Chief Warden is Dr. Sunita Bhosale (fictional). Email: hostel@xyz.edu.example.

## Hostel blocks

| Block | Residents | Rooms | Capacity | Warden (fictional) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Boys, second to fourth year | 200 | 400 | Mr. Deepak Joshi |
| B | Boys, second to fourth year | 180 | 360 | Mr. Anil Kapoor |
| C | Girls, all years | 160 | 320 | Dr. Rekha Nambiar |
| D | Boys, first year | 150 | 300 | Mr. Vinod Shetty |

All rooms are double sharing with a bed, study table, chair and cupboard for each resident. Room numbers start with the block letter, for example A-214 is room 214 in Block A.

## Hostel fee

- Room rent: Rs 30,000 per semester.
- Mess charges: Rs 24,000 per semester (Rs 4,000 per month).
- Total hostel fee: Rs 54,000 per semester, added to the semester bill.
- One-time refundable caution deposit: Rs 10,000.
- Full details are in the Fee Structure document.

## Allotment

- First-year students get priority. Rooms are allotted on distance from home (more than 50 km gets priority) and then on merit.
- Room allotment for senior students is done every year in July based on CGPA.
- Applications for hostel seats for the Even semester 2026-27 close on 2026-12-05.
- A student with unpaid hostel dues for more than 30 days may lose the hostel seat.

## Timings

- In-time: 22:00 for all residents. Late entry after 22:00 must be recorded at the gate with a reason.
- Three late entries in a month lead to a written warning, and a parent is informed.
- Night out or weekend leave needs warden approval on the hostel app at least 12 hours in advance.
- Visitors (parents and guardians only) may meet residents in the visitors lounge from 09:00 to 19:00. Visitors are not allowed inside rooms.

## Mess

- The central mess serves breakfast (07:30-09:30), lunch (12:00-14:00), snacks (17:00-18:00) and dinner (19:30-21:30).
- Vegetarian and non-vegetarian options are available. The menu is set by the student mess committee every month.
- Mess rebate: residents on approved leave for 5 or more consecutive days get a rebate of Rs 120 per day.
- Special diet on medical advice is available on request to the warden.

## Facilities

- Wi-Fi in all rooms (100 Mbps per block, fair use policy)
- 24x7 hot water, RO drinking water on every floor
- Laundry service twice a week (Rs 600 per semester, optional)
- Common room with TV, indoor games and a reading room in each block
- Gym in Blocks A and C, open 06:00 to 09:00 and 17:00 to 21:00

## Prohibited items and fines

| Violation | Fine |
|---|---|
| Electric heater, induction cooktop or iron in the room | Rs 1,000 and the item is confiscated |
| Damage to hostel property | Repair cost plus Rs 500 |
| Unauthorised guest in the room | Rs 2,000 |
| Smoking or alcohol | Rs 5,000 and disciplinary action |
| Ragging | Expulsion from the hostel and the college |

## Anti-ragging

Ragging is a criminal offence and is strictly banned. The anti-ragging helpline (fictional) is +91-20-5550-0199, available 24x7. Complaints can also be emailed to antiragging@xyz.edu.example.

## Vacating the hostel

Residents must vacate rooms during the summer vacation. Room keys are returned to the warden, and the caution deposit is refunded within 30 days of final vacating, minus damages.
