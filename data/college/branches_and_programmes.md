# Branches and B.Tech Programmes

XYZ College of Engineering (fictional) offers a four-year B.Tech in four branches. Every programme has 8 semesters and needs 160 credits to graduate. The first year (Semesters 1 and 2) is common for all branches. All names in this document are fictional.

## Common first year

First-year students of every branch study the same Semester 1 courses: MA101 Engineering Mathematics I, PH101 Engineering Physics, CS101 Programming for Problem Solving, ME101 Engineering Graphics and HS101 Communication Skills, plus a workshop practice lab. Branch-specific courses start from Semester 3.

## Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

- Intake: 120 seats (plus 12 lateral entry seats)
- Head of Department: Dr. Kavita Rao (fictional)
- Department email: hod.cse@xyz.edu.example
- Accreditation: accredited by the (fictional) National Board of Engineering Quality till 2029

### CSE curriculum highlights

- Semester 3: Data Structures, Digital Logic Design, Object Oriented Programming, Discrete Mathematics
- Semester 5: Operating Systems, Database Management Systems, Computer Networks, Theory of Computation, Elective I
- Semester 7: Compiler Design, Cloud Computing, Information Security, Elective III, major project phase 1
- A 6-week industry internship is compulsory after Semester 6.

### CSE labs

Programming Lab (120 systems), Data Science and AI Lab with 8 GPU workstations, Networks and Security Lab, Cloud Computing Lab, and an Open Source Software Lab.

### CSE electives

Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Blockchain Basics, Computer Vision, Big Data Analytics and Human Computer Interaction.

## Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)

- Intake: 90 seats (plus 9 lateral entry seats)
- Head of Department: Dr. Suresh Iyengar (fictional)
- Department email: hod.ece@xyz.edu.example

### ECE curriculum highlights

- Semester 3: Network Analysis, Electronic Devices, Signals and Systems, Probability and Random Processes
- Semester 5: Digital Signal Processing, Analog Communication, Microprocessors and Microcontrollers, Control Systems, VLSI Design
- Semester 7: Wireless Communication, Embedded Systems, Optical Communication, Elective III
- A 6-week industry internship is compulsory after Semester 6.

### ECE labs

Electronic Devices Lab, Communication Systems Lab, VLSI Design Lab with EDA tool licences for 40 users, Embedded and IoT Lab, and an RF and Microwave Lab.

### ECE electives

IoT Systems, Satellite Communication, Image Processing, Radar Engineering, Mixed Signal Design and Machine Learning for Signal Processing.

## Mechanical Engineering (ME)

- Intake: 60 seats (plus 6 lateral entry seats)
- Head of Department: Dr. Ramesh Choudhary (fictional)
- Department email: hod.me@xyz.edu.example

### ME curriculum highlights

- Semester 3: Engineering Thermodynamics, Strength of Materials, Manufacturing Processes, Numerical Methods
- Semester 5: Heat Transfer, Design of Machine Elements, Fluid Machinery, Dynamics of Machinery, Elective I
- Semester 7: Finite Element Analysis, Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, Operations Research, Elective III
- A 6-week industry internship is compulsory after Semester 6.

### ME labs

Thermal Engineering Lab, CNC and Advanced Manufacturing Lab, CAD/CAM Lab with 60 workstations, Fluid Machinery Lab, Robotics Lab, and the Formula Student workshop.

### ME electives

Automobile Engineering, Robotics, Additive Manufacturing, Renewable Energy Systems, Industrial Engineering and Composite Materials.

## Civil Engineering (CE)

- Intake: 60 seats (plus 6 lateral entry seats)
- Head of Department: Dr. Anjali Deshpande (fictional)
- Department email: hod.ce@xyz.edu.example

### CE curriculum highlights

- Semester 3: Surveying, Fluid Mechanics, Building Materials and Construction, Numerical Methods
- Semester 5: Structural Analysis, Geotechnical Engineering, Transportation Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Elective I
- Semester 7: Design of Steel Structures, Estimation and Costing, Water Resources Engineering, Elective III
- A 6-week site internship is compulsory after Semester 6.

### CE labs

Surveying Lab with total stations and drones, Concrete and Materials Testing Lab, Geotechnical Lab, Environmental Engineering Lab, and a Transportation Engineering Lab.

### CE electives

Remote Sensing and GIS, Earthquake Engineering, Green Buildings, Construction Project Management, Bridge Engineering and Smart Cities.

## Minor and honours

Students with a CGPA of 8.0 or more and no backlogs can register from Semester 5 for an Honours degree in their own branch (20 extra credits) or a Minor in another branch (18 extra credits). Popular minors are Data Science (offered by CSE) and IoT (offered by ECE).

## Branch change

Branch change is allowed once, after the first year. The rules (CGPA of at least 8.5, no backlogs, and seat vacancy) are in the Academic Rules document.
