# About XYZ College of Engineering and Admissions 2026-27

All information in this document is synthetic and describes a fictional college.

## About the college

XYZ College of Engineering is a fictional, autonomous, co-educational engineering college located at Plot 12, Knowledge Park, Nandipur (a fictional city). The college was established in 1998 and offers a four-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree in four branches:

- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
- Mechanical Engineering (ME)
- Civil Engineering (CE)

The campus covers 42 acres and has 4 hostel blocks (A, B, C and D), a central library, a placement cell, a sports complex with a cricket ground and an indoor badminton hall, and a 24x7 health centre. The Principal is Dr. Harish Malhotra (fictional). The college follows a semester system: the Odd semester runs from July to December and the Even semester from January to May.

## Eligibility

- Passed Class 12 (or equivalent) with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, plus one of Chemistry, Computer Science, Biology or a vocational technical subject.
- At least 60% aggregate marks in Physics, Mathematics and the third subject (55% for SC/ST candidates).
- A valid score in the XYZ Common Entrance Test (XCET 2026) or a JEE Main 2026 percentile.
- Age: no upper age limit.

## Admission process

1. Register online on the admissions portal between 2026-04-01 and 2026-05-15. Application fee: Rs 1,200 (Rs 600 for SC/ST).
2. Write XCET 2026 on 2026-05-24 (computer-based, 150 questions, 3 hours). JEE Main candidates may skip XCET.
3. The merit list is published on 2026-06-12. Merit is based on 70% weightage to the XCET (or JEE Main percentile) and 30% to Class 12 PCM marks.
4. Counselling rounds: Round 1 on 2026-06-20, Round 2 on 2026-06-30, spot round on 2026-07-08.
5. Report to campus with documents and pay the first installment of fees. First-year reporting days are 2026-07-15 to 2026-07-17. Classes begin on 2026-07-20.

## Student ID and college email

Every admitted student receives a student ID in the format XYZ + two-digit admission year + branch code + three-digit roll number. For example, XYZ24CSE001 is CSE roll number 001 admitted in 2024, and XYZ25ME007 is ME roll number 007 admitted in 2025. The student ID is used to log in to the student portal, the library and the Smart Campus Assistant. Every student also gets a college email ID on the domain xyz.edu.example.

## Admission cancellation

A student who wants to cancel admission must apply in writing to the Admissions Office. Refunds follow the refund policy in the fee structure document. Original certificates are returned within 7 working days of the cancellation request.
