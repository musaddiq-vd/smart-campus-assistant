# Fee Structure 2026-27

This is the fee structure of XYZ College of Engineering (fictional) for the academic year 2026-27. Fees are charged per semester and are the same for all four branches (CSE, ECE, ME, CE). All amounts are in Indian Rupees (Rs).

## Academic fee per semester

| Component | Open / General | OBC-NCL and EWS | SC / ST |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuition fee | 80,000 | 60,000 | 20,000 |
| Development fee | 8,000 | 8,000 | 8,000 |
| Lab and library fee | 4,000 | 4,000 | 4,000 |
| Examination fee | 3,000 | 3,000 | 3,000 |
| Total academic fee per semester | 95,000 | 75,000 | 35,000 |

The examination fee of Rs 3,000 per semester covers the mid-semester and end-semester examinations. Supplementary examinations are charged separately (Rs 500 per course).

## Hostel and mess fee per semester

| Component | Amount per semester |
|---|---|
| Hostel room rent (double sharing) | 30,000 |
| Mess charges (Rs 4,000 per month x 6 months) | 24,000 |
| Total hostel fee per semester | 54,000 |

A one-time refundable hostel caution deposit of Rs 10,000 is paid at the time of first hostel allotment. It is billed separately and refunded when the student leaves the hostel, after deducting any damages.

## Total semester bill

The semester bill shown on the student portal is the academic fee plus the hostel fee (only for hostel residents).

| Category | Day scholar (per semester) | Hostel resident (per semester) |
|---|---|---|
| Open / General | 95,000 | 1,49,000 |
| OBC-NCL and EWS | 75,000 | 1,29,000 |
| SC / ST | 35,000 | 89,000 |

## Total yearly cost

One academic year has two semesters (Odd and Even), so the yearly cost is two semester bills.

| Category | Day scholar (per year) | Hostel resident (per year) |
|---|---|---|
| Open / General | 1,90,000 | 2,98,000 |
| OBC-NCL and EWS | 1,50,000 | 2,58,000 |
| SC / ST | 70,000 | 1,78,000 |

A first-year Open category hostel resident pays Rs 2,98,000 for the year plus the Rs 10,000 caution deposit, which is Rs 3,08,000 in total. The full four-year B.Tech for an Open category day scholar costs Rs 7,60,000 in academic fees.

## Payment schedule for Odd semester 2026-27

Fees can be paid in full or in two installments.

| Installment | Amount | Due date |
|---|---|---|
| Installment 1 | At least 50% of the semester bill | 2026-08-14 |
| Installment 2 | The remaining balance | 2026-10-31 |

For the Even semester 2026-27, Installment 1 is due on 2027-01-15 and Installment 2 on 2027-03-31.

## Fee deferment

A student who is waiting for an education loan or a scholarship disbursal can apply to the Accounts Office for a fee deferment before the Installment 2 due date. If approved, the Installment 2 due date moves to 2026-11-30 and no late fee is charged until then. Only one deferment is allowed per semester.

## Late fee

- A late fee of Rs 100 per day is charged for every day after the due date until the dues are paid.
- The late fee is capped at Rs 5,000 per semester.
- For the Odd semester 2026-27, the late fee on Installment 2 starts on 2026-11-01. A student who has not paid by 2026-11-03 owes a late fee of Rs 300 (3 days).
- Students with unpaid dues on 2026-11-25 will not be able to download the end-semester hall ticket until the dues and late fee are cleared.

## Payment modes

- UPI (any UPI app) on the student portal: no extra charge
- Net Banking: no extra charge
- Debit card: no extra charge
- Credit card: 1% convenience fee
- NEFT/RTGS to the college account (details on the portal); upload the UTR number within 2 days
- Demand draft in favour of "XYZ College of Engineering", payable at Nandipur

Cash is not accepted. Every payment generates a receipt with a number like XYZR-2026-01234, visible on the student portal. Scholarship credits applied directly to the fee account get a receipt number starting with XYZS.

## Scholarships and fee concessions

Approved scholarships are credited directly to the student's fee account and reduce the amount the student has to pay. Details of every scholarship are in the Scholarships document.

## Refund policy

| When the student withdraws | Refund |
|---|---|
| Before classes begin (before 2026-07-20) | Full fee minus Rs 1,000 processing charge |
| Within 15 days after classes begin (by 2026-08-04) | 50% of the tuition fee; other fees not refunded |
| After 2026-08-04 | No refund of tuition or other academic fees |

Hostel rent is refunded pro rata only for medical withdrawal. Unused mess advance is refunded for full months not used. The caution deposit is always refunded, minus damages.
