# Placements Report 2026

Campus placement results of XYZ College of Engineering (fictional) for the B.Tech batch that graduated in 2026 (placement season July 2025 to May 2026). All companies named here are fictional. Training and Placement Officer: Mr. Rajiv Khanna (fictional), email placements@xyz.edu.example.

## Overall summary

- Students eligible for placement: 307
- Students placed: 253
- Overall placement rate: 82.4%
- Overall average package: Rs 7.67 LPA (lakh per annum)
- Highest package: Rs 32 LPA (CSE)
- Companies that visited: 68
- Total offers made: 281 (some students received more than one offer under the dream offer rule)

## Placement eligibility and policy

- Minimum CGPA of 6.0 at the time of registration.
- No active backlogs. Students with backlogs can register only after clearing them.
- At least 75% attendance in the training sessions run by the placement cell.
- One student, one offer: after accepting an offer, a student can apply only for a "dream offer" with a package at least 1.5 times the current offer.
- A student who skips a company's interview after being shortlisted is barred from the next 3 companies.

## Placement season 2026-27

Registration for the batch graduating in 2027 (current fourth-year students, Semester 7) opened on 2026-07-25 and closed on 2026-08-10. Pre-placement talks started on 2026-08-20. Campus interviews continue through the Odd and Even semesters. Students must keep their resume updated on the placement portal.
