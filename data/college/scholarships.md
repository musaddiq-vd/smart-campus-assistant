# Scholarships 2026-27

Scholarships and fee concessions at XYZ College of Engineering (fictional). All scholarships are per semester unless stated otherwise. Approved scholarship amounts are credited directly to the student's fee account (receipt numbers start with XYZS) and reduce the fee the student pays. Scholarship Cell email: scholarships@xyz.edu.example.

## Summary of scholarships

| Scholarship | Amount per semester | Who can apply | Seats |
|---|---|---|---|
| XYZ Merit Scholarship | Rs 20,000 (25% of the Open tuition fee) | Top 5% by SGPA in each branch and year | About 16 per year group |
| XYZ Need-based Scholarship | Rs 30,000 | Family income below Rs 4,00,000 per year | 40 |
| SC/ST Tuition Scholarship | Rs 20,000 (full SC/ST tuition fee) | SC and ST students with family income below Rs 8,00,000 | All eligible students |
| Girls in STEM Scholarship | Rs 15,000 | Women students with CGPA 8.0 or more (first year: top 25% in XCET) | 30 |
| XYZ Sports Scholarship | Rs 10,000 | Students who represented the state or the nation in a sport | 15 |

## XYZ Merit Scholarship

- Awarded to the top 5% of students by SGPA in the previous semester in each branch and year.
- The SGPA must be at least 9.0, with no backlogs.
- First-year students are selected by XCET 2026 rank (top 10 ranks in the college).
- Renewed every semester if the student stays in the top 5%.

## XYZ Need-based Scholarship

- For students whose total family income is below Rs 4,00,000 per year (income certificate required).
- The student must have at least 75% attendance and no more than 1 active backlog.
- Can be combined with the Sports Scholarship but not with the Merit Scholarship.

## SC/ST Tuition Scholarship

- Covers the full SC/ST tuition fee of Rs 20,000 per semester.
- Family income must be below Rs 8,00,000 per year.
- The student still pays the development fee, lab and library fee, examination fee and hostel fee.
- An SC/ST day scholar with this scholarship pays Rs 15,000 per semester (Rs 35,000 bill minus Rs 20,000).

## Girls in STEM Scholarship

- For women students in any branch with a CGPA of 8.0 or more.
- First-year women students qualify if they are in the top 25% of XCET 2026 rank among admitted women students.
- Can be combined with any other scholarship.

## XYZ Sports Scholarship

- For students who represented the state or the nation in a recognised sport in the last two years.
- The student must take part in inter-college tournaments for the college.

## Rules for combining scholarships

- A student can receive at most two scholarships at the same time.
- The total scholarship cannot be more than the student's tuition fee for the semester.
- The Merit and Need-based scholarships cannot be combined.

## How to apply

1. Apply on the student portal under "Scholarships" between 2026-07-20 and 2026-08-10 for the Odd semester (Even semester: 2027-01-04 to 2027-01-25).
2. Upload the income certificate, category certificate or sports certificates as needed.
3. The Scholarship Committee announces results within 15 days of the closing date.
4. Approved amounts are credited to the fee account. If the student already paid the full fee, the excess is refunded to the student's bank account within 30 days.

## Loss of scholarship

A scholarship is cancelled if the student is found guilty of malpractice or ragging, or gives false documents. The amount already credited must then be paid back.

## Education loans

The college has tie-ups with (fictional) partner banks for education loans of up to Rs 10,00,000 without collateral. Students waiting for loan disbursal can request a fee deferment to 2026-11-30 (see the Fee Structure document).
