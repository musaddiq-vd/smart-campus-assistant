## Student portal

The student portal is used for fee payment, attendance, results, hall tickets, library account and scholarship applications. Students log in with their student ID (for example XYZ24CSE001) and password. For a password reset, write to ithelp@xyz.edu.example from the college email ID.

## Grievances

Academic grievances go to the Dean Academics. Fee grievances go to the Accounts Office. If a grievance is not resolved within 15 days, the student can write to the Student Grievance Committee at grievance@xyz.edu.example.
